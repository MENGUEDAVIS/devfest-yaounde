# Community wall — what we keep, and how to take a card down

This is the short note linked from the DP generator. The public page is
`/{locale}/wall/terms`.

## What is stored

When you download, share or copy a card, we save **a smaller copy of the
composed card** (about 640px, JPEG). Not the photo you picked from your
camera roll. No GPS, no camera serial — the card is drawn on a canvas and
re-encoded on the server.

We also keep the display name you typed, the time, the style you chose, and
the wording of the notice you were shown.

Cards stay up for **200 days**, then the image and the row are deleted.

## Where it appears

On the public Community Wall (`/wall`), unless you take it down or an
organiser hides it. The wall is `noindex` — it is not offered to search
engines.

## How to take a card down

Write **gdgyaounde@gmail.com** and say which nickname or which picture.
There is no report button on the wall. An organiser will hide or delete it.

Do not put a card of a child on the wall. If one appears, write that same
address.
